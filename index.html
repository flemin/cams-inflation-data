Federal Reserve Analysis: Why All-Items CPI Follows Services & Shelter, Not Traded Goods

Notice how All-Items CPI (+140%) closely tracks Food at Home (+139.8%), while apparel, furnishings, and electronics remained flat or plummeted. You might ask: If so many consumer items are deflationary, why is overall inflation so high?

The Federal Reserve & BLS weight consumer spending categories by their share of the average household wallet. Traded physical goods (clothing, TVs, laptops, appliances) make up less than 15% of total CPI weight. Conversely, Shelter (Rent & Owner's Equivalent Rent) accounts for ~36% of the entire CPI weighting, Healthcare accounts for ~8%, and Non-Traded Services (auto repair, tuition, insurance) account for over 20%.

Because domestic services, rent, and healthcare cannot be offshored to Asian factories, their price inflation surged (+350% to +800%). The All-Items CPI line is pulled upward by non-traded services and shelter, matching Food at Home because grocery prices are 85% domestic US service and labor costs!

The Logistics Evolution: Megaships, Flat Shipping Costs & The Death of Brick-and-Mortar

1. Shipping Supply Matched Massive US Demand: Between 1990 and 2024, US container import volume from Asia exploded by over 340% (from ~5 million TEU to 22+ million TEU). Yet despite this massive surge in consumer demand, baseline shipping rates remained remarkably flat (~$1,500 to $1,950 per container). This is an extraordinary engineering achievement: maritime shipping capacity via Megaships (4,000 TEU → 24,000 TEU) expanded in lockstep with Asian manufacturing demand, keeping freight costs per item at near-zero baseline ($0.05 per shirt, $1.50 per TV).

2. Shipping Was Never The Cost Bottleneck: Ocean freight was never the reason retail prices were high. Historically, a $5 factory item in China was marked up 400% to $25 in US stores to cover US commercial rent, store clerks, regional distribution centers, and unsold inventory write-downs. The price was high due to domestic US middleman markup.

3. Temu, Shein & Direct Factory Dropshipping: Modern direct-from-China platforms (Temu, Shein, AliExpress) bypassed US domestic warehouses entirely. By utilizing direct air-parcel logistics and leveraging the US De Minimis tax exemption ($800 duty-free threshold), items ship straight from Chinese factory floors to US doorsteps—delivering the "pure" factory price without middleman retail markup and undercutting physical retail stores by 50% to 80%.

The Physics of Money: Inelastic Supply & Global Population Ratio

Unlike fiat currencies which can be expanded infinitely by central banks, physical gold and silver cannot be deflated or arbitrarily scaled. Gold and silver mining is constrained by geology and energy input: as high-grade surface deposits are exhausted, mining companies must process significantly more tons of rock at deeper depths to extract each additional ounce of metal, continually increasing marginal extraction costs.

Furthermore, annual global gold mining output increases total above-ground gold stocks by only ~1.5% per year—matching long-term global human population growth. Total mined gold equates to approximately 0.8 troy ounces per human being on Earth. This fundamental mathematical supply ceiling is what makes precious metals the ultimate non-debasable store of value over centuries.
